Recipe

Prep time: Servings: 2 - 3

Ingredients
Item Qty.
Large Head of Broccoli 1
Salt 2 tsp
Garlic powder 3 tsp
Paprika 3 tsp
Black pepper 2 tsp
Olive Oil 2 tbsp

Directions

  1. Preheat your airfryer to 400F
  2. Rinse and dry your broccoli thoroughly before separating into small chunks.
  3. Combine your dry ingredients in a large freezzer ziplock bag. Then, add your oil.
  4. Add the broccoli pieces into the ziplock, zipping it shut and shaking it to evenly spread the seasonings.
  5. In the airfryer basket, spread the broccoli apart evenly, leaving room for space in between for air to circulate.
  6. Let it cook in the airfryer for 7 minutes.
  7. Serve as a side with a dish of choice. Enjoy!
