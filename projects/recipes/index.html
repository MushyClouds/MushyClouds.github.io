About This Page

Welcome to my recipe page! Here, I want to share some recipes that I like to cook for my family. I'm not a professional at cooking, nor do I ever want to become one, I just like to cook for my family because amidst all the chaos, I like that I could calm everyone with a good tasting meal at the end of the day. There are only two recipes here, which is a perfectly short amount for a well-curated collection not alot, but we make do with what we have. Quality over quantity?

Maybe there will be something here that you would like to cook at home, but who knows. I do have one message though if you aren't a fan of cooking: you don't have to enjoy it or have it be a serious hobby, you just have to understand which stuff tastes good together. That's how I got started after all!
