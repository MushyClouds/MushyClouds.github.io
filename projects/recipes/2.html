Recipe

Ingredients
Item Qty.
Large Russet Potatos 6
Flour 1 Cup
Paprika 2 tbsp
Garlic powder 2 tbsp
Ground Dried Oregano 1 1/2 tbsp
Ground Black Pepper 1 1/2 tbsp
Chilli Powder 1 1/2 tbsp
Onion Powder 1 1/2 tbsp
Ground Black Pepper 1 1/2 tbsp
Shredded Cheese 2 Cups
(Reccomended): Add a small amount of salt to taste

Directions

Prep time: Servings: 6

  1. Boil your potatoes on high until soft, then strain and set aside in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate smaller bowl, combine your seasonings and flour.
  3. Mash the potatoes until no chunks remain. Then, fold in all your dry ingredients, includings your cheese.
  4. Oil a large pan and set to medium heat. This is the perfect time to take the dough and shape it into small discs to set aside until the pan is hot.
  5. Pan fry both sides until crispy.
  6. Serve as a side with a meal of your choice, or as an appetizer. Enjoy!
